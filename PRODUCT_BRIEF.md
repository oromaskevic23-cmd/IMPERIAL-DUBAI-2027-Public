# Product brief — Governed Invoice Exception Agent

**IMPERIAL Core · Dubai 2027 · Independent competition preparation**

## Business problem

Finance teams need to compare invoices with purchase orders and supplier records, identify discrepancies and retain accountability for decisions. An advisory model alone cannot grant payment authority.

## Proposed value

The prototype demonstrates a bounded workflow: reconcile structured data, identify an exception, route it to an authorized human reviewer, record the decision and retain integrity-verifiable evidence. An owner stop can block new submissions. No payment is authorized or executed.

| Stakeholder | Intended benefit | Evidence required before a commercial claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finance operator | Less repetitive reconciliation work | Timed, paired human baseline |
| Reviewer | Explicit exception context and review | Correct decision rate and review time |
| Security and audit | Bounded authority and traceable decisions | Independent access-control and integrity acceptance |
| Owner | Persistent stop control | Operational recovery and availability tests |

## Demonstrated synthetic scenario

The disclosed local benchmark contains 24 synthetic cases, including 12 reviews and zero false matches in that run. It does not measure customer savings or real-world model generalization. Read [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) for the signed checkpoint.

## Acceptance roadmap

1. Improve model quality after the first [actual local inference experiment](REAL_MODEL_RESULTS.md): transport and safety exercised, quality acceptance failed.
2. Execute independent security and browser acceptance.
3. Run an authorized paired pilot with predeclared cases, measured human time and costs.
4. Review the evidence, limitations and commercial readiness before deployment.

No production SLA, customer relationship, prize, funding, endorsement or registration acceptance is claimed. The implementation remains private.
