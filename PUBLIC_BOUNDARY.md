# Public / Private Boundary

This repository is the independent public communication surface of IMPERIAL Core's Dubai 2027 competition preparation.

## Approved public content

Product overview, high-level architecture principles, approved artwork, attributed branding and scoped aggregate development evidence. Public statements must distinguish targets from verified implementation.

## Excluded from public disclosure

Original competition implementation, private repositories and internal links, credentials, keys, tokens, customer data, raw operational logs, internal infrastructure details, deployment configuration and unpublished research. No automated mirroring from the private development repository is permitted.

## Release governance

Review material for intellectual property, competition eligibility, confidentiality, accurate evidence and third-party rights before publication. No claim of Dubai Chamber partnership, endorsement, admission, award or financing without authoritative confirmation. Reference artwork is contextual and does not authorize use of third-party marks beyond applicable permissions.
